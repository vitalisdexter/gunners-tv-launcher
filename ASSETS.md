# Artwork and asset provenance

Exact filenames and SHA256 hashes are in ASSET-MANIFEST.json. Fjord/Dusk and their separated layers were generated upstream. Silver/Graphite silk and Silk are upstream Blender renders. Milky Way, Calm Sea and Dawn derive from CC0 Poly Haven panoramas Rogland Clear Night, Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky and Kiara 1 Dawn. Thumbnails derive from the same set. Soft navigation sounds are synthesized by the upstream generator. zones.txt derives from the public-domain zone.tab. These origins are documented at the upstream revision recorded in PROVENANCE.md; all copied bitmap/sound hashes are checked against that reference. No font is bundled.

The cannon/football icon and geometric GTV banner are original vector artwork created for this derivative; offered under MIT. They are not the official Arsenal crest. Runtime club/provider logos are fetched from their providers or installed apps and are not bundled in source. No private viewing-history posters are packaged. Demo illustration is generated from original shapes and synthetic labels, not an actual device screenshot.
